# Patagonia Skimo

PATAGONIA SKIMO — LANDING PAGE

Premium Brand Landing (Lovable Prompt)

Overview

Design a premium landing page for Patagonia SkiMo.

This is NOT an ecommerce.

This is NOT an online shop.

The purpose of this website is to build trust, communicate expertise, and convert visitors into WhatsApp conversations.

The visitor should leave the page thinking:

"These guys already did all the research. I just need to ask them what equipment is right for me."

Brand Positioning

Patagonia SkiMo is not a retailer.

It is a curated selection of premium mountain equipment.

The founders are two Argentine friends living in Switzerland.

One lives in Verbier, in the heart of the Alps.

The other lives in Bern.

They spend the year researching, comparing and sourcing the best opportunities across Europe.

They don't try to sell everything.

They only bring products they genuinely believe in and would personally use.

Their value is not low prices.

Their value is expertise, curation and trust.

Core Message

We don't sell equipment.

We curate mountain equipment.

Everything begins with our own criteria.

Target Audience

Argentinian skiers.

Ski touring enthusiasts.

Splitboard riders.

Freeriders.

People who appreciate premium equipment.

Intermediate to advanced mountain users.

Customers investing significant money in equipment and looking for expert advice before buying.

IMPORTANT DESIGN RULES

DO NOT:

 Create an ecommerce homepage.

 Use gradients.

 Use glassmorphism.

 Use neumorphism.

 Use excessive animations.

 Use oversized icons.

 Use mountain illustrations.

 Use colorful cards.

 Use generic outdoor website templates.

 Use dark backgrounds.

 Overload sections with text.

 Make products the main focus.

Instead:

Design an editorial experience.

Every section should communicate one clear idea.

Trust should come before products.

Products should feel like the consequence of Patagonia SkiMo's expertise—not the reason for its existence.
